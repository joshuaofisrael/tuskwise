  • Thailand seizes 3.1 tonnes of ivory hidden in timber from Zambia

    30 October 2026

    Thai customs found 469 African elephant tusks and tusk pieces, over 3.1 tonnes of ivory in total, sealed in wax inside hollowed timber shipped from Zambia to Laem Chabang port.

  • Why don't elephants get cancer?

    30 October 2026

    Elephants do get cancer, but far less often than their size suggests. About 5% of elephants die of it versus 11 to 25% of people, likely thanks to 20 copies of the TP53 gene and cells quick to self-destruct.

  • Are elephants afraid of bees?

    28 October 2026

    Yes. African and Asian elephants move away from the sound of disturbed honey bees, and African elephants even give a special alarm rumble for bees. Farmers now use beehive fences to keep elephants out of crops.

  • How many teeth do elephants have?

    27 October 2026

    Elephants grow 26 teeth in a lifetime: two tusks and 24 cheek teeth, six in each half of each jaw. Only one or two cheek teeth per side are in use at once, replaced from behind like a conveyor belt.

    25 October 2026
