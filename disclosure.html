Editorial and AI disclosure

TuskWise publishes original educational pages about elephants. This page explains who writes them, how images are labelled, and how advertising stays separate from the writing.

How we write

Every page is written for TuskWise. We do not copy text from other sites. Facts come from published sources such as the IUCN Red List, WWF, accredited zoos and peer reviewed studies, and each guide lists those sources. We are not elephant researchers or veterinarians. Where evidence is thin or scientists disagree, we say so. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact form.

AI-generated images

Some images are AI-generated illustrations made to look like realistic wildlife photographs. Each one is labelled as an AI-generated image in its alt text or caption. They show typical elephant scenes, not a real individual animal, a real person or a real event, and we do not use them to illustrate news stories. Real photographs are credited on the photo credits page. More detail is in About our images.

Advertising and editorial independence

TuskWise may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve those ads. Advertising does not change which facts we report or how we report them, and we do not sell editorial placement. You can opt out of personalized advertising at https://adssettings.google.com. Cookie details are in our privacy policy.
