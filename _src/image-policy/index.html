AI-generated illustrative images

Many of the photo-style images on TuskWise are AI-generated. They are not photographs of a real elephant, a real person, or a real event. They are illustrations made to look like realistic wildlife photographs, used beside our articles on elephant anatomy, behavior, and habitats. Every AI-generated image carries a caption that says the image is an AI-generated illustrative image, and that caption's Source link points to this page.

Real, licensed photographs

Other pages use real photographs of elephants, taken by named photographers and published under Creative Commons licences on Wikimedia Commons. Each real photograph is credited in its own caption with the photographer's name and licence, and every real photograph on the site is listed with its author, licence, and a link to the original file on our photo credits page.

How to tell the two apart

Read the caption under any photo-style image. If it says the image is AI-generated, it is an illustration, not a photograph of a real elephant or event, and its Source link leads here. If the caption names a photographer and a Creative Commons licence, it is a real photograph, and the same photograph is listed on the photo credits page.

Diagrams and games

TuskWise also uses a small number of original line diagrams, such as the side-profile comparison on the African vs Asian elephant page, plus three small browser games. These are original illustrations and interactive pages built for TuskWise. They are not photographs and are not AI-generated images.
