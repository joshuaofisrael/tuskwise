What this game teaches

  • African savanna elephants have the largest ears and both sexes usually grow tusks.
  • African forest elephants are smaller, with straighter, more downward tusks and rounder ears.
  • Asian elephants have smaller ears, a single finger-like tip on the trunk, and tusks mainly in some males.
