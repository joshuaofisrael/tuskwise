Accuracy of information

We write every page from published sources such as the IUCN Red List, WWF, accredited zoos, universities and peer reviewed studies, and we list those sources on each page. Even so, research is updated, population estimates are revised and mistakes can happen. Information may be incomplete or out of date by the time you read it. If you spot an error, please tell us and we will review it.

No professional relationship

TuskWise is not written by veterinarians, wildlife biologists, travel safety experts, lawyers or financial advisers, and reading the site or contacting us does not create any professional, advisory or client relationship. Our content is general educational information, not veterinary, medical, travel safety, legal or financial advice.

Ethical travel guidance is general

Our ethical viewing guide and sanctuary checklist describe practices to look for and avoid, based on published welfare guidance. They do not rate, endorse or accuse any specific venue, and conditions at any venue can change. Always verify a venue yourself before you book: read its current policies, look for independent accreditation, and check recent independent reports. You are responsible for your own travel decisions and safety, and you should follow local laws and official travel advice.
