Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Analytics

We use, or plan to use, Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is privacy focused: it does not use cookies or local storage to track you and does not collect personal data for advertising. We use it only to see which pages are useful.

Contact form and email

Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your name, email address and message to our inbox. We use these details only to read and, where appropriate, reply to your message. We do not add you to any mailing list. If you email us directly, the same applies.

Advertising and affiliate links

TuskWise currently shows no ads and contains no affiliate links. If that changes, we will update this policy and label any such content clearly.

External links

Our pages link to sources such as the IUCN, WWF, zoos and scientific journals. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
