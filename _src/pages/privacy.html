Who is responsible for your data

TuskWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can reach us at joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

Contact form and email. Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your name, email address and message to our inbox. If you email us directly we receive the same kind of information. We use these details only to read and, where appropriate, reply to your message. We do not add you to any mailing list and we do not sell or share your details for marketing.

That is the only personal information we collect. The site has no accounts, no sign up, no comments and no chat.

Analytics

TuskWise uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first, cookieless analytics service that doesn't use cookies or track visitors across sites. It counts page views and basic technical details such as the page visited, referring site, browser and country so we can see which pages are useful. It does not use local storage to track you, does not build a profile of you and does not use that data for advertising. Advertising is described separately in the Advertising section below. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

Cookies and local storage

TuskWise itself does not set cookies and uses no affiliate tracking cookies. Google and its partners may set advertising cookies, as described in the Advertising section below. If you play a game on the site, your best score may be saved in your own browser's local storage so it is there next time; it never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so loading a page does not contact any font service.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Advertising

TuskWise uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage your ad settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom see a consent choice, delivered through Google's consent message, before ad cookies are used. TuskWise contains no affiliate links. We will mark any sponsored content clearly (see our Disclaimer).

Children

TuskWise is a general audience educational site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the contact form, email us and we will delete it.
