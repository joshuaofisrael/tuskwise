Who is responsible for your data

TuskWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can reach us at joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

Contact form and email. Our contact form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your name, email address and message to our inbox. If you email us directly we receive the same kind of information. We use these details only to read and, where appropriate, reply to your message. We do not add you to any mailing list and we do not sell or share your details for marketing.

That is the only personal information we collect. The site has no accounts, no sign up, no comments and no chat.

Analytics

We do not currently run any analytics on TuskWise. We plan to add Cloudflare Web Analytics in the future to count page views. It is privacy focused: it does not use cookies or local storage to track you and does not collect personal data for advertising. We will update this page when it is switched on.

Cookies and local storage

TuskWise does not set cookies. We use no advertising cookies (no Google AdSense or similar) and no affiliate tracking cookies. If you play a game on the site, your best score may be saved in your own browser's local storage so it is there next time; it never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so loading a page does not contact any font service.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Advertising and affiliate links

TuskWise currently shows no ads and contains no affiliate links. If that changes, we will update this policy and our Disclaimer and label any such content clearly.

Children

TuskWise is a general audience educational site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through the contact form, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any message you have sent us by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

External links

Our pages link to sources such as the IUCN, WWF, zoos and scientific journals. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
