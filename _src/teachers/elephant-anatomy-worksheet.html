Label the elephant

Word bank: trunk, tusk, ear, eye, leg, tail

  1. The trunk is a nose and upper lip joined together. It has no bones. Name one thing an elephant does with it: _______________
  2. A tusk is a tooth. About one third of it sits hidden inside the skull. True or false? _______________
  3. Big ears are full of blood vessels. Flapping them helps the elephant _______________

Learn more on the elephant anatomy pagetuskwise.com/anatomy. Figures there come from San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance and the Smithsonian's National Zoo.

Answer key (for the teacher)

A: eye. B: ear. C: tusk. D: trunk. E: tail. F: leg.

  1. Any of: breathe, smell, eat, drink, pick things up, spray water or dust, greet other elephants.
  2. True.
  3. Cool down (shed heat).
