Matriarch's Footsteps

The matriarch knows the way to water. Watch her footsteps light up, then tap the same pads in order. Each round grows longer, just as a calf learns routes by following the herd.

Round 1Score 0Best 0
Press Start, watch the path, then tap the pads in the same order. Keyboard: number keys 1 to 9 match the pads left to right, top to bottom.

What this game teaches

  • Elephant families are led by an older female called the matriarch.
  • Herds remember routes to waterholes and seasonal food over many years.
  • Calves learn by following: path memory is a life skill, not a party trick.
