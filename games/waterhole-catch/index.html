Waterhole Catch

An elephant needs food and water every day. Move left and right to catch falling fruit and water with the trunk. Skip the thorns. A short fact card appears when the round ends.

Score 0Lives 3Best 0
Press Play. Drag, swipe, or use arrow keys / A and D to move. Sound stays off until you turn it on. Reduced motion shortens the round.

What this game teaches

  • Elephants are herbivores: grasses, leaves, bark, roots and fruit.
  • An adult may drink tens of litres of water a day when it is available.
  • The trunk is a multi-tool for drinking, smelling, touching and picking food.
