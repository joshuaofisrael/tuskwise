# Free teacher resource directories for TuskWise (research only, nothing submitted)

Checked 8 Oct 2026. **No accounts created and nothing submitted.** Every option that needs an account is blocked until Joshua says yes (rule: no new accounts without his approval). Paid options are excluded unless Joshua approves spending.

Licence note: TuskWise is currently "All rights reserved". Several OER libraries expect an open licence (for example CC BY NC). Joshua would need to decide whether to put the printables, or only those, under an open licence before submitting to those libraries.

| # | Directory | What it is | Account needed? | Free? | Submission URL / method | Fit and notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OER Commons (ISKME) | Large public library of open educational resources, K-12 and higher ed | Yes, free account | Yes | Log in, then Create > Submit from Web. Help: https://help.oercommons.org/support/solutions/articles/42000046849-submit-oer | Strong fit for /teachers/ printables and /research/. Collection policy expects OER, so needs an open licence decision. Reviewed before listing. |
| 2 | Smithsonian Learning Lab | Smithsonian platform where teachers build and share collections; external URLs can be added | Yes, free account (https://learninglab.si.edu/auth/create) | Yes | Create a collection, Upload a Resource, "I want to contribute a website / URL". Help: https://learninglab.si.edu/help/create/adding-and-customizing-resources | Good fit: an "Elephants: free classroom resources" collection could include TuskWise pages beside Smithsonian's own elephant material. |
| 3 | MERLOT | Curated OER directory run by California State University, strongest for higher ed | Yes, free membership (https://www.merlot.org/merlot/join.htm) | Yes | Add menu, "Add a Material to MERLOT", enter URL. Help: https://info.merlot.org/merlothelp/Add_a_Material.htm | Best fit is /research/ for undergraduates. Resource must be open online material with its own URL. |
| 4 | Share My Lesson (AFT) | Free lesson sharing community for pre K to 12 educators | Yes, free account | Yes | "Upload a Lesson" after login, with optional standards tagging (https://sharemylesson.com/) | Upload the worksheets as files with a link back. Fits K to 12 teachers directly. |
| 5 | Tes | Large UK and international teaching resource marketplace with free sharing | Yes, free account | Yes for free resources | Author Dashboard > Add resource, choose "share for free". Guide: https://www.tes.com/author-academy/begin-your-resource-author-journey/walking-you-through-uploader | Free resources must take a Creative Commons licence (CC BY SA is the default), so this needs the licence decision. Good for UK teachers. |
| 6 | Curlie | Human edited web directory (successor to DMOZ) | No account to suggest a site | Yes | Find the single most specific relevant category on curlie.org (an elephant or mammals category under Kids and Teens or Science; exact category not yet chosen), check that it shows the suggest icon, then use it. Instructions: https://curlie.org/docs/en/add.html | Low effort, low traffic, but a clean editorial link. Submit once only. Still counts as a form submission, so needs Joshua's yes. |
| 7 | CK-12 Foundation | Free K-12 STEM content platform | No account for email submission | Yes | Email a Word file to submissions@ck12.org with description, grade and course (https://info.ck12.org/share-your-content) | Accepted work is published under CC BY NC SA. Email submission counts as outreach: needs Joshua's yes and his licence decision. |

Excluded:
- Teachers Pay Teachers: the first upload must be free, but a seller account is needed and TPT lists a fee for its Basic Seller tier. Paid, so not without Joshua's yes.
- Common Sense Education: reviews edtech products, and its product review submissions are currently paused. Not a fit.
- PBS LearningMedia and National Geographic Education: no open submissions.

## Top 5 (recommended order once Joshua approves accounts)
1. Smithsonian Learning Lab: free account, no licence change needed for linking.
2. Share My Lesson: free account, K to 12 teachers.
3. OER Commons: free account, needs open licence decision.
4. Curlie: no account, one suggestion.
5. MERLOT: free account, for /research/ and higher ed.
